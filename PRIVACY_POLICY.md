# Privacy Policy for Downpour

**Last updated:** January 30, 2026

**Developer:** Ascensciana

## What Downpour Does
Downpour is an emotional release journaling app. You write what's on your mind, then release it.

## Data We Collect
**None.** Downpour does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on external servers.

## Data Stored On Your Device
Your journal entries (text, emotion tags, and timestamps) are stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. This data:
- Never leaves your device
- Is not accessible to us or anyone else
- Is not backed up to any cloud service

## Analytics & Tracking
Downpour contains **no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.**

## Deleting Your Data
You can delete individual entries or clear all data at any time from within the app (Archive → Clear all).

## Age Requirement
Downpour is intended for users aged 13 and older.

## Third-Party Services
Downpour does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data.

## Changes to This Policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.

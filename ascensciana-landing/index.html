Philosophy

I believe technology can hold space for what matters. Not more features, but more meaning. Apps that invite you to pause, feel, and move through moments with intention.

The Story

I'm Larra. I started building because I needed something that didn't exist — tools that feel like rituals, not chores. Downpour began as a personal experiment: what if releasing thoughts could feel as cathartic as rain clearing a storm?

Current Project

Downpour

A ritual for letting go. Write what weighs on you, tag your emotion, and release it. Watch your words dissolve into rain as the storm clears. No journals to maintain. No streaks. Just closure.
